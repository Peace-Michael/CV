About Me

Data Analyst • Business Intelligence Analyst • MSc Data Science

I am Peace Michael — a Data Analyst and Business Intelligence professional with an MSc in Data Science. I transform complex data into meaningful business insights using analytics, cloud technologies and machine learning.

I am passionate about solving business problems with data-driven decision-making and creating scalable analytical solutions. With a strong foundation in statistics and modern machine learning, I bridge the gap between raw data and strategic business outcomes.

I am equally comfortable designing robust data pipelines in the cloud as I am presenting executive-level insights to senior stakeholders. My approach combines rigorous analytical thinking with clear, compelling communication.

I thrive in environments where data is at the heart of the product strategy — building systems that are scalable, maintainable, and genuinely impactful.
